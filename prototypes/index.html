Separate environments

Membership is not administration.

The future ShadowBridge Command Center belongs to the owner and explicitly authorised administrators. C.A.R.T.E.L. Division remains a separate invitation-only strategic network. Neither partner status nor C.A.R.T.E.L. membership grants Command Center privileges.

Private access, identity disclosure, approval and suspension require secure backend authorisation and audit logs. No such capabilities are active in these designs.
